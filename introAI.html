Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Introduction to Artificial Intelligence is now in its third year at SSIS. This course explores the fascinating world of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) while fostering critical thinking, problem-solving, and technical skills. Students gain hands-on experience with AI and ML tools, applying their learning to real-world solutions. Course content will encompass the following Units:

  1. 1) Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
  2. 2) Basics of Python for AI
  3. 3) AI in Creative Fields
  4. 4) Introduction to Machine Learning
  5. 5) Deep Learning & Neural Networks
  6. 6) Natural Language Processing
